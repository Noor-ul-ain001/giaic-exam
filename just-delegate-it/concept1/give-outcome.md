# Concept 2: Give AI the Outcome, Not the Clicks

---

## The Core Idea

There are **two ways** to hand over the same job:

| | **Recipe** | **Outcome** |
|---|---|---|
| **What you write** | Step-by-step instructions | One sentence: what should exist |
| **Who plans the work** | You | AI |
| **Who owns the method** | You | AI |
| **Risk** | Your plan is the limit | AI finds the best path |

---

## The Two Approaches — Side by Side

### Recipe (What Most People Write)
```
Look only at Coursera and Udemy. Find the three highest-rated
courses on each for learning Python. For each course copy the
name, the price, and the length into a table. Then tell me
which is best.
```

### Outcome (What You Should Write)
```
When this is done, I will have a table of three Python courses
I can start this week, each with a name, price, and length.
```

---

## What Just Changed?

### The Problem with the Recipe

- AI followed **your steps** — including the weak ones
- You named two sites — AI **never looked beyond them**
- If better courses existed elsewhere, **the table will not show them**
- "Highest-rated" came from **you** — if the course page shows no rating, AI invented one
- If AI refused a step, **you had to fix the method yourself**

> Your plan was the ceiling. The result could only be as good as your recipe.

### Why the Outcome Wins

- AI chose **where to look** — not limited to sites you named
- You judged the result against **what you asked for**
- Not against a plan you invented under pressure
- Better sources were possible because **you did not lock the method**

---

## An Important Note on Steps

> Steps still have a place — but **only when a source or order of work is genuinely required.**

That is a **constraint** — and Concept 3 gives constraints their own line.

**What you should not do:** write steps because you feel nervous.

```
Nervous feeling → belongs at the END (when you verify)
                  NOT at the START (when you delegate)
```

## Do It Now

**Step 1:** Open a fresh chat.

**Step 2:** Run the recipe version with two providers from your Concept 1 table:

```
Look only at [provider one] and [provider two]. Find the three
highest-rated courses on each for learning [your subject]. For each
course copy the name, the price, and the length into a table.
Then tell me which is best.
```

> If AI says it cannot browse those sites — tell it to **search them instead** and continue.

**Step 3:** Put this result next to your Concept 1 table.

**Step 4:** Ask yourself:

- Did the recipe stay **inside only the sites you named** — even if better options existed?
- Where did **"highest-rated"** come from? Is it shown on the course page, or did AI supply it?
- Did the outcome version use **sources you did not name?**
- Which result could you judge **without trusting your own plan first?**

---

## Verify Before You Accept

Find **one disagreement** between the two results:

| What to Look For | Example |
|-----------------|---------|
| A different **price** | One says $14.99, the other says free |
| A different **length** | One says 10 hours, the other says 6 |
| A **"free"** one says and the other does not | |

Open the course page and settle it.

> If the two tables share **no course at all** — that is the finding.
> The recipe never saw what the outcome version found.
> In that case, check **one price from each table** instead.

**Remember:** Being right had nothing to do with which message was longer.

## Your Turn

Write the outcome for your running job as **one sentence** beginning:

```
When this is done, I will have...
```

Then add **two limits**, one line each:

```
When this is done, I will have a shortlist of three courses I can
use to learn [your subject] from zero.

Limit 1: Must be possible to start this week.
Limit 2: No more than five hours a week.
```

Run this in a fresh chat.

---

## Check Your Work

- [ ] Does my sentence describe a **result**, not an action?
- [ ] Could someone else read it and know **when the job is finished?**
- [ ] Did I leave the **method to AI?**

> If any answer is **no** — rewrite the sentence before continuing.

---

## Remember

> Say what should **exist** when the job is done.
> Leave the clicks to the machine. Keep the judgment for yourself.

**Check yourself:**
*Should your message describe the result or the steps — and why?*
