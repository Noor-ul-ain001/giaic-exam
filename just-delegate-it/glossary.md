# The Delegation Loop — Simple Explanation

## What is This About?

This is a **framework for working with AI effectively** — how to give AI a task and manage it well, from start to finish.

---

## The Delegation Loop (6 Steps)

Think of it like a **cycle you repeat every time you give AI a job.**

```
Define → Delegate → Observe → Intervene → Verify → Accept
  ↑                                                    |
  └─────────────── (repeat if rejected) ───────────────┘
```

### 1. Define
You clearly state **what result you want** before anything begins.

### 2. Delegate
You **hand the job to AI** and give it responsibility for producing the result.

### 3. Observe
You **step back and let AI work** — no need to direct every single step.

### 4. Intervene
If AI drifts or goes wrong, you **correct, redirect, constrain, or clarify.**

### 5. Verify
You **check the claims, numbers, and actions** that actually matter.

### 6. Accept
You **decide** — is the result fit for use, or does it go back?

---

## The Delegation Brief

> A work order you fill out **before AI starts.** Six questions. Not a form — a job description.

| # | Question | What It Means |
|---|----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Outcome** | What should exist when the work is complete? |
| 2 | **Context** | What does AI need to know that it cannot know on its own? |
| 3 | **Constraints** | What requirements or limits apply to this job? |
| 4 | **Authority** | What can AI decide without asking you? |
| 5 | **Deliverable** | What form should the result take — table, document, file? |
| 6 | **Verification** | What will you check before accepting? *(Write this before the work starts)* |

## Key Terms

### Fresh Chat
A **new conversation** — nothing from the previous chat carries over. Clean slate every time.

### Running Job
One task you practice throughout the course, **delegated again at every concept** with one more detail added each time.

### Need First, Then Tool
You learn a control or setting **only when a job actually needs it** — not in advance.

### Plan-First
Ask AI to **list its steps and wait for your approval** before it begins.

## Projects

A **Project** is a workspace that holds files and rules, shared across every chat inside it.

| Term | What It Means |
|------|---------------|
| **Project** | A space holding your files and standing instructions |
| **Knowledge** | Files AI may read in every chat inside the project |
| **Standing Instruction** | A rule written once that AI follows in every chat |

---

## Temporary / Incognito Chat

A chat that is **not saved** and does not affect memory or history.

- ChatGPT calls it → **Temporary Chat**
- Claude calls it → **Incognito Chat**

## Verify — Inside the Step

Verification has **four inner steps:**

```
Identify → Trace → Challenge → Decide
```

And ends in **one of four decisions:**

| Decision | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Accept** | The output is good — use it |
| **Correct** | Fix a small issue, then proceed |
| **Investigate** | Something is unclear — dig deeper first |
| **Reject** | The output is wrong or unfit — send it back |

### Review Thresholds

These are the **four questions** that decide whether you must verify carefully.  
**One crossed is enough** to put it on the must-check list.

1. **Stakes** — What happens if this is wrong?
2. **Reversibility** — Can this action be undone?
3. **Audience** — Who is receiving this output?
4. **Regulatory Exposure** — Is there legal or compliance risk?

---

## Delegation Record

A **one-page record** of a completed job — four parts:

```
Brief → Run → Check → Decision
```

| Part | What It Captures |
|------|-----------------|
| **Brief** | What you asked for |
| **Run** | What AI did |
| **Check** | What you verified |
| **Decision** | What you decided — accept, correct, or reject |
